# Future validation work

Before adding voice link-budget predictions, reliability percentages, or calibrated good/bad thresholds, obtain real paired WSPR and voice-link observations over representative paths and conditions.

Record UTC time, endpoints, frequency, antennas, receiver noise/interference, reported and independently checked WSPR power, and the actual voice waveform and transmit-power definition (including SSB PEP versus average power). Separate ALE link establishment/LQA from sustained MELP speech usability and SSB intelligibility. Include unsuccessful attempts and repeated observations across receivers, days, seasons, and power levels to avoid successful-contact bias.

Use independent observations to evaluate any proposed relationship and its uncertainty before exposing predictions to operators. Until then, keep the app focused on historical WSPR evidence normalized to a fixed WSPR reference power.

This is a future research note. No operator logging workflow or data collection is enabled now.
